How to keep a watchlist with Stackime

Use Stackime to remember where you stopped and what to watch next. Discovery is open to everyone; saving a list requires Google or email sign-in.

  1. Find a title. Browse the discovery rails or choose Browse all to search and filter AniList's catalog. The community catalog shows titles that viewers here have saved.
  2. Save your place. Choose Add title, select the anime, then set its status, episodes seen and optional score. For example, a 12-episode series at episode 4 belongs under Watching with 4 episodes seen.
  3. Return to My list. Update your progress, move a finished series to Completed, or remove an entry. Opening a watcher from a title's detail page shows their public list.

Your list contributes to public community views. AniList supplies artwork, descriptions and its own scores; Stackime supplies the tracking tools, viewer progress and community ratings. It does not stream episodes.

If discovery is unavailable, try its retry button or use the community catalog. If the catalog is also unavailable, return later; this walkthrough remains readable without either service. See how account and list data are used.

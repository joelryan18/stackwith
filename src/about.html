01 / WHO

HEADCOUNT: 1

Joel Ryan. One builder. No fictional team.

Joel Ryan designs, codes, tests, writes, and maintains stackwith.me. The domain is a personal software workshop rather than an agency or venture-backed product company. Stackime, Deep Signal, Signal Strike, and the site itself are working browser projects; AXON is an explicitly labelled product-interface study.

That distinction matters. A working interaction is described as working. A proposed agent capability is described as a design target. The field notes point to implementation details, trade-offs, and limits so readers can judge the work without relying on a polished landing-page claim.

03 / ORIGIN

LOGGED 03:14 A.M.

It started at 3:14 a.m.

AXON began as a design question: what would an automation product look like if its interface treated auditability as the central feature rather than a settings-page afterthought? The 3:14 a.m. framing is a narrative device for that question, not a claim about a deployed AXON incident.

The showcase turns the idea into an inspectable interface: sense, reason, act, and explain. The page demonstrates the visual system, interaction design, checkout for optional supporter passes, and a simulated trace. It does not provide the autonomous-agent backend depicted by the concept.

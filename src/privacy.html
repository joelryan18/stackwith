Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information stackwith.me ("we", "us") collects when you visit this website (https://stackwith.me), how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and honest — the same tolerance we hold our product to.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us — if you contact us or request access, we store the email address you submit so we can respond.
  • Usage data — standard server logs and analytics: pages viewed, approximate location (country/city level), device and browser type, and referring pages.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — small files stored by your browser, described in detail below.

2. Cookies we use

We use the following categories of cookies on stackwith.me:

Essential cookies

These are required for the site to function and cannot be switched off.

Name Purpose Duration
axon-consentRemembers your cookie-consent choice so the banner is not shown on every visit.Until cleared
game-view, game-mode, game-loadout, ab-soundRemembers your preferences inside individual browser projects (view angle, game mode, weapon loadout, sound on/off). Never sent to a server.Until cleared

Analytics cookies

These help us understand how the site is used so we can improve it. They are set only after you accept non-essential cookies.

Name Purpose Duration
_ga, _ga_*Google Analytics — aggregated traffic and engagement data.2 years

Advertising cookies (Google AdSense)

These are set by Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-7262404901375077) and its advertising partners to serve relevant ads. They are active only after you accept all cookies. With "Essential only" selected, Google serves non-personalised ads and these cookies are not set.

Name Purpose Duration
IDEDoubleClick — used by Google to register and report your actions on the site after viewing or clicking an advertisement.1 year
test_cookieChecks whether your browser supports cookies.Session
NID, DSIDGoogle ad personalisation and frequency capping.6 months

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings, the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page, or by selecting "Essential only" in our cookie banner (which denies ad_storage and ad_personalization under Google Consent Mode v2).

3. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve this website.
  • To respond to access requests and inquiries you send us.
  • To measure traffic and performance in aggregate.
  • To display advertising where you have consented to it (served via Google AdSense).

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us run the site (hosting via GitHub Pages, analytics via Google Analytics, advertising via Google AdSense, payments via Razorpay, database via Supabase), or when required by law.

5. Data retention & security

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete it. Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with access controls.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@stackwith.me — we respond within 30 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area, you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact privacy@stackwith.me and we will delete it.

8. Third-party services

stackwith.me loads content from third-party services whose own privacy policies apply:

  • Google (AdSense, Analytics, Fonts)
  • Supabase — powers the Stackime community catalog and optional account login
  • Razorpay — processes optional supporter-pass payments
  • AniList — provides anime data to Stackime via GraphQL (no personal data sent)

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be flagged on the homepage.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@stackwith.me, or see our contact page.
